What is McTimoney Chiropractic?

McTimoney Chiropractic is a gentle and precise approach to chiropractic care designed to restore correct alignment and balance to the musculoskeletal system. Using light, rapid adjustments, this refined method helps to reduce pain, ease tension, and improve mobility without the need for forceful manipulation.

At Spine and Core Synergy, we specialise in the McTimoney chiropractic technique, offering a holistic approach to spinal health that treats your entire body as an interconnected system. This method uses light, precise adjustments to restore balance and alignment throughout your skeletal structure.

The McTimoney Technique: Whole Body Balance

The McTimoney technique is particularly well-suited to those seeking gentle healthcare solutions, providing effective treatment without forceful manipulation. Our approach focuses on gentle, swift movements that encourage your body's natural healing processes while addressing the root causes of discomfort.

This method is widely valued for its ability to address common concerns such as back, neck, and joint pain, as well as postural imbalances and restricted movement, all while supporting your body's natural movement and healing capacity.
